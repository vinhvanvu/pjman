## Decision

An actor is a person or an agent; every record an adapter writes carries its writer; owners and authorities may be either. Where a human is required is policy on the tree, not a property of the system; day one, every scope requires a human. Agents coordinate through the record, never through messages. A decision is authored by an actor with authority for its scope, and humans set which scopes require a human.

## Why

A factory of agents needs accountability per actor without inventing identities; the human line moves as autonomy grows and the model does not change.
