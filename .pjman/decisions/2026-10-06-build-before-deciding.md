## Decision

Decisions are made over grounded variants with stated trade-offs; forcing questions are surfaced before the build. An ask is bounded: a recommended answer, a deadline, a default.

## Why

Deciding over prototypes beats deciding over documents when building is cheap; bounded asks keep questions from queuing.
