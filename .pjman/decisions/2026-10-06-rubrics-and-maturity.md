## Decision

An owner's or authority's criteria are versioned and run by agents on every change beneath. Maturity is a claim plus graded evidence (telemetry > simulation > analysis); a level is a rubric plus the grade of evidence it requires. pjman stores citations and confidence, never the data.

## Why

Maturity as a label is a rubber stamp; as evidence it is checkable.
