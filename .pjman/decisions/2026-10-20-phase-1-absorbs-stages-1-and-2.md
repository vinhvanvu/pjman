## Decision

Phase 1 now ends when pjman works for the founder's own use and is portable enough that one more person can install it and get value. Its remaining steps are: run pjman's own development from the store; build the founder's mods and user interfaces from the store, replacing the Phase 0 work tool's hand-maintained views; make the store and adapter portable to a second person. Hosting and selling (stage 3) are not planned until those two are in place.

## Why

Stages 1 and 2 fold into Phase 1 without changing its shape: the tool, the store and the adapter exist, and views over them are the natural next step. Stage 3 is the stretch; its shape depends on what the first two teach.
