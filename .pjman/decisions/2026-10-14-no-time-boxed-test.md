## Decision

Phase 1 step 4, the eight-week test with a build-or-kill rule, is dropped. There is no time frame. Success is reached in stages and each one counts on its own: pjman's own development runs on this foundation; custom mods and user interfaces are built from the store; the store is hosted as shared context and sold as a product. Reaching all three is secondary to going through the process and learning. The four events the test would have tallied (a superseded decision honoured, a decision not found when needed, a write the validator refused, a decision proposed before the work) remain worth noting in session logs when they happen, as evidence, with no threshold attached.

## Why

A deadline and a kill rule frame the work as a bet to be settled; the founder frames it as a process to be learned from, where each stage is a success in itself. The milestones are the real gates: the second user and a paying customer are external checks that a self-graded tally is not.
