## Decision

The thesis. AI agents made building software cheap and competent; they did not make agreeing on what to build cheap. pjman is organised around alignment, not execution.

## Why

Code was the high-skill blocker in front of the product; removing it exposed the problem every engineer has lived with: collecting context, finding the right people, getting decisions out of them.
